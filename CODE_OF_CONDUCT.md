# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Evergrow to be a calm, welcoming project. Everyone should be able to
participate without harassment, discrimination or hostility.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient and constructive.
- Assume good intent and critique ideas, not people.
- Respect different experience levels, identities and viewpoints.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking or unwanted attention.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Insults, personal attacks or sustained disruption.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions
and private communication about the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to **[CONDUCT CONTACT — TO BE DECIDED]**. Reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers may warn, remove content,
or temporarily or permanently ban participants.
